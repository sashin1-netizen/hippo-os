# Hippo OS — Living Customisation Contract

## Product rule
Everything the user experiences should feel configurable, but the sanctuary must remain alive. Customisation changes appearance, environment, camera feel, accessibility and baseline temperament. It does not reduce the animals to scripted puppets or erase memories, learned preferences, boundaries or species-specific behaviour.

## User-customisable layers

### Animals
- Names.
- Body build within healthy species-appropriate limits.
- Skin/coat warmth and visual treatment.
- Pattern/marking strength where biologically plausible.
- Eye presentation.
- Baseline curiosity, social tolerance and play-energy bias.
- Interaction preferences are not hard-set; animals can still learn different preferences through lived experience.
- Species identity remains locked: Mochi is a pygmy hippo, Truffle is a pig, Bao is a Chinese Shar-Pei.

### Sanctuary
- Vegetation density.
- Water clarity/presentation.
- Mud amount.
- Lighting warmth.
- Living-weather intensity.
- Wind activity.
- World-motion intensity.
- Auto-living-world toggle.
- Habitat enrichment and layout customisation is required before launch.

### Interface
- Accent hue.
- Glass/depth treatment.
- Interface scale.
- Text/accessibility settings.
- Stats visibility.
- Audio levels and haptics.

### Camera
- Cinematic, Caretaker, Bodycam and Overhead POVs.
- Bodycam motion intensity.
- Reduced-motion stabilization.
- Camera sensitivity and reset.

## Living-world requirements
- Real device IANA timezone remains the clock source.
- Day/night follows the real local time by default.
- Wind, humidity, cloudiness, dampness, water activity and ambient wildlife activity continue evolving while the app is open.
- Offline progression updates needs and memory without death or punitive neglect mechanics.
- The world stores its customisation state in the versioned sanctuary save.
- User changes are applied live and persist through restart.
- Animals keep autonomous decision-making after customisation.
- Learned food, touch, zone and activity preferences remain independent of user-set baseline temperament.
- Cross-species relationships, familiarity and preferred distance must evolve over time rather than being hard-coded friendships.
- Wetness, mud, water, weather, foliage movement, tracks and enrichment wear are visual launch gates, not optional post-launch ideas.

## Quality principle
"Everything customisable" does not mean exposing unsafe or biologically absurd values. Sliders are bounded to species-appropriate visual and behavioural ranges. The user shapes the sanctuary and each animal's baseline character; the simulation decides what happens next.
