# Hippo OS Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026

Hippo OS is designed as a private, offline virtual sanctuary.

## Data collection

Hippo OS does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social-login SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioural profiling.

## Local app data

Animal names, needs, preferences, bond, journal events, settings, and progression are stored locally on the device in the app's private storage. This information is used only to restore the sanctuary state and does not leave the device through Hippo OS.

Removing the app may remove local sanctuary data unless the operating system preserves application data.

## Permissions

Hippo OS may use vibration/haptics for tactile feedback. The app does not require camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone, SMS, storage, or advertising identifier permissions for the core experience.

## Children

Hippo OS is not designed to collect personal data from children or adults. If the app is later distributed through an app store, the selected target audience and content rating must accurately reflect the released build and store listing.

## External services

The launch build is intended to operate without a network connection. If a future version adds optional online functionality, this policy must be updated before that feature is released.

## Changes

Material privacy changes will be reflected in a new version of this policy and in the app's About/Privacy screen.

## Contact

Support and privacy contact details should be supplied in the final store listing before public distribution.
