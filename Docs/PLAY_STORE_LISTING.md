# Hippo OS — Google Play Listing Draft

## App name
Hippo OS

## Short description
A private virtual sanctuary with three deeply simulated animal companions.

## Full description
Hippo OS is a private virtual sanctuary built around three individual animal companions: a baby pygmy hippo, a pig, and a Chinese Shar-Pei.

Each animal has its own species-specific behaviour, needs, temperament, preferences, memories, routines, and relationship with you. The animals can explore, rest, forage, investigate, accept or reject interaction, develop preferences, and continue their routine while you are away.

The sanctuary includes individual habitat zones, day and night behaviour, feeding and interaction, a persistent journal, camera controls, haptics, sound controls, and local save progression.

Hippo OS is designed around observation and companionship rather than punishment loops. There are no ads, no paid energy systems, and no account is required for the core experience.

Key features:
- Baby pygmy hippo, pig, and Chinese Shar-Pei
- Species-specific autonomous behaviour
- Persistent individual temperament and bond
- Feeding and interaction preferences
- Sanctuary journal and memories
- Offline progression
- Dynamic day and night presentation
- Animal, ambience, UI and master sound controls
- Haptics and reduced-motion options
- Local private save data
